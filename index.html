Hey there! I’m Annie Chang, a product designer with a focus on UX and visual design. I’m currently studying Human-Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington, where I’ve developed a strong foundation in user experience, interaction design, and visual storytelling. My work is guided by empathy and simplicity, and I enjoy transforming complex ideas into thoughtful, intuitive experiences that make people’s lives easier and more delightful.

Outside of design, I love exploring cafes for matcha, taking photos that capture small details of everyday life, and rewatching my favorite Studio Ghibli films. Whether I’m designing or simply observing the world, I’m always inspired by the moments that make life feel warm, intentional, and human.
