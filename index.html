About

Hi there! I'm Annie

I'm a product designer with a focus on visual communication and user research. I’m currently studying → Human-Centered Design & Engineering at the University of Washington, where I’ve developed a strong foundation in user experience, interaction design, and visual storytelling. My work is guided by empathy and simplicity, and I enjoy transforming complex ideas into thoughtful, intuitive experiences.

Outside of design, I love exploring cafes for good grassy matcha, taking photos that capture small details of everyday life, and rewatching my favorite Studio Ghibli films. Whether I’m designing or simply observing the world, I’m always inspired by the moments that make life feel warm, intentional, and human <3
